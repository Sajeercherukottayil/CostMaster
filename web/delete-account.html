Delete Your Account

Last updated: 26 October 2026

You can delete your CostMaster account at any time. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

Delete it yourself

  1. Open the CostMaster app, or go to costmaster-ck.web.app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are logged out.

Or ask us to delete it

If you can't log in, email mohammedsajeerck@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my CostMaster account". We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your login: your name, email address and password.
  • Your Premium, including a lifetime purchase or a running 15-day pass. Premium is not refunded when you delete your account.
  • Your profile and test history saved on the device you delete from. On other devices they are removed when you uninstall the app or tap Reset app.

What is kept

Records of payments you made (the plan, amount, date and payment ID) are kept for up to 8 years, as Indian tax and accounting law requires. They are no longer linked to your name or email address. Google Play and Razorpay keep their own payment records under their privacy policies.
