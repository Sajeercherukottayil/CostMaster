Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the CostMaster app and website collect, why, and how it is handled. CostMaster is run by Cherukottayil Mohammed Sajeer, Vengara, Malappuram, Kerala 676304, India.

Information we collect

Account details
Your email address and password, used to create your account and log you in. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
Profile and practice data
Your name, course, group and target exam term, and your test attempts and scores. These are stored on your device only. They are not sent to our servers.
Premium and payment records
When you buy Premium, we record your account ID, the plan, the amount, the Razorpay order and payment IDs, the date, and when your Premium ends. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not receive or store your card number, UPI ID or bank details.

We do not use advertising, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and log you in.
  • To take payments and unlock Premium on your account.
  • To answer your questions and help with payment problems.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

We do not sell or rent your information.

Who we share it with

Only with the services that run CostMaster, for the purposes above:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for accounts and Premium records.
  • Razorpay to process payments.
  • Vercel, which hosts our payment server.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Storage and security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Premium records can be read only by the account they belong to, and can be changed only by our payment server.

How long we keep it

Account details and Premium records are kept while your account exists. Payment records may be kept longer where tax or accounting law requires. Data on your device is removed when you reset or uninstall the app.

Your choices and deleting your account

You can see and change your profile in the app. You can delete your account and the data linked to it in the app under Profile > Delete account, or by email; see Delete your account. Deleting your account also ends any Premium on it.

Children

CostMaster is meant for students preparing for professional examinations. If you are under 18, use it with the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
