How to install

  1. Tap Download the app and open the downloaded file (CostMaster.apk).
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. CostMaster is not on the Play Store yet, so Google Play Protect may warn about an unknown developer: tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open CostMaster and create your account.

Already installed? Download and install again to update. Your account and Premium stay the same.

What's free and what's Premium

Free: chapter study and chapter tests for every paper.

Premium unlocks all Mock Tests and Full Paper Tests: ₹49 for a 15-day pass or ₹149 for lifetime, paid once in the app by UPI or card. See Pricing.
