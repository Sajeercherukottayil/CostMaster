Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

No refunds

CostMaster Premium is a digital service that is activated on your account immediately after payment. All payments for Premium (the ₹49 15-day pass and the ₹149 lifetime plan) are final and non-refundable, including when Premium is not used, or is used only in part.

Chapter study and chapter tests are free, so you can try CostMaster before you buy.

Cancellation

Premium plans are one-time payments, not subscriptions. Nothing renews automatically and there is nothing to cancel. A 15-day pass simply ends after 15 days.

Payment problems

We will help where something has gone wrong:

  • Charged but Premium not unlocked: if Premium is not active within 24 hours of a successful payment, contact us with your payment ID and the email address of your account. We will activate it.
  • Charged twice for the same purchase: we will refund the duplicate payment.
  • Money deducted but the payment failed: Razorpay or your bank normally returns the amount automatically within 5–7 working days. If it does not, contact us and we will help.

Any refund we agree to is made to the original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days.
