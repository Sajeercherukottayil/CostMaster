# cost_master

CostMaster - CMA India student companion app

## Question content rules (ICMAI)

CostMaster is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by The Institute
of Cost Accountants of India (ICMAI). To stay clear of ICMAI's copyright and
trademarks:

- **Write every question yourself.** Do not copy or lightly reword questions,
  illustrations or solutions from ICMAI study material, RTPs, MTPs or past
  exam papers. Use the official syllabus only to decide *which topics* to cover.
- **Don't use the ICMAI logo**, and don't describe the app as "official",
  "approved" or "ICMAI mock test". Keep the in-app disclaimer on the home screen.
- **Avoid content that goes stale fast.** Taxation questions depend on the
  Finance Act in force for each exam term. Add them only with the applicable
  assessment year in the question text, and review them every term.
- **Check every answer before adding it.** Each question needs exactly four
  options, one correct `answerIndex` and an explanation showing the working.
  `flutter test` checks the structure, but not that the answer is correct.

Questions live in `lib/data/questions/`, one file per subject, and are combined
in `lib/data/question_bank.dart`.
